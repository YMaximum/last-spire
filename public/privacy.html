Privacy and save information · updated 19 October 2026

You can play as a guest without Google sign-in. Guest progress stays in the browser. Cloud saving is optional and is available only when the game’s account service is enabled.

Guest saves and device settings

The game stores progress in this browser using IndexedDB. Audio, graphics, window layouts, and interface preferences stay on the device. Separate, optional gameplay observations also stay in the browser; you can export or delete them in Settings → Local gameplay data. They are not uploaded with cloud saves.

Enemy encounter and defeat counts are part of your saved progress. They synchronize only with your optional account checkpoint; the bestiary does not send a separate tracking feed.

Optional Google identity

Opening the account sign-in interface loads Google’s identity library to show its sign-in button. Google handles its sign-in screen under Google’s privacy policy. Last Spire receives a short-lived identity proof, verifies it on the save server, and uses the stable Google identifier and a display name to identify your game account.

Last Spire does not receive your Google password, request Gmail or Drive access, or store Google access or refresh tokens. Email and picture claims are not stored in the account database. The identity proof is used for verification and is not kept as an account credential.

What cloud saving stores

If you deliberately adopt an account checkpoint, the save server stores permanent progress, unlocks, credits, and the active expedition, including its build, random-generator state, queued choices, and reward-settlement record. Up to five prior cloud revisions are retained for recovery. Account data is kept until you delete the game account.

Guest and account progress are separate. The game does not automatically add currencies together or overwrite conflicting saves. A device takeover requires a choice; unsynced progress remains in that device’s account cache.

Sessions and request protection

The service uses an essential, first-party, HttpOnly session cookie. Production cookies are Secure and SameSite Strict. A signed-in session can last up to 30 days; sign-out revokes that session. Short-lived sign-in sessions expire after ten minutes. No advertising or third-party analytics cookies are added by Last Spire.

The server uses IP addresses temporarily in memory to limit abusive requests. Hosting infrastructure may process ordinary connection information to deliver the website. Save payloads, Google proofs, and authentication cookies must not be written to application logs.

Export, sign-out, and deletion

  • Export your current save from Settings or Account & cloud saves. Treat exported files as personal progress data.
  • Signing out returns to the guest save. The account’s local cache is retained so unsynced progress can be recovered.
  • Account & cloud saves → Delete account removes the live account, its cloud saves and history, and all server sessions. A recent Google sign-in and typed confirmation are required.
  • Deletion also removes that account’s cache and recovery copies from the current browser. Other devices’ offline copies cannot be erased remotely; clear their site data yourself if needed.
  • Database recovery copies can retain deleted data for up to seven days. They are not live saves. A private deletion journal retains only random internal account IDs, without names, Google identifiers, or progress, to prevent recovery from restoring deleted accounts.

Clearing browser site data removes guest and cached account progress, settings, and local logs on that browser. Export anything you want to keep first.

Optional Endless rankings

Guests can browse rankings. Publishing requires an active signed-in account, a separate public username, a complete result preview, and explicit confirmation for each result. Syncing your save never publishes a score. Your Google name and email are never automatically used as your public username.

A published entry shows your chosen username, score, fully cleared waves, time to the last clear, tower, evolution path, and summarized rune/fusion loadout. It does not reveal your Google identifier, email, internal account or run IDs, full save, or private gameplay logs. Rankings are Casual · client-reported, without prizes or replay verification.

The save service retains bounded completed-result receipts for publication and retries. Credits are settled independently. You can withdraw an entry in My results & username; changing your username also renames existing entries. Deleting your game account removes its public username, results, and rankings. The recovery-copy and deletion-journal limits described above also apply to leaderboard data.

Recovery limits

The hobby save service currently keeps recovery copies on the same server. They can help recover a bad checkpoint, but cannot protect against total server loss, disk loss, or compromise. Keep an export on another device if your progress matters to you. Off-site storage is not currently included.

Questions

For account or privacy questions, use the developer support contact shown on the Google sign-in screen. Do not post identity proofs, session cookies, or personal saves in public issues.
